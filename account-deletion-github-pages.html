Account & Data Deletion

LogLogic · Developer: Xu Xiuzhen · Last updated: June 11, 2026

This page explains how to delete your LogLogic account and associated data on Android (Google Play) and iOS. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Before you delete: If you have an active subscription, cancel it first in Google Play (Profile → Payments & subscriptions) or the App Store (Settings → Subscriptions). Deleting the app or your account does not cancel billing automatically.

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open LogLogic and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab (bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

2. Request deletion by email (if you uninstalled the app)

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the same email address registered on your LogLogic account. We will reply with a confirmation step to verify ownership before any deletion is processed.

wszh1995@gmail.com

Subject line: LogLogic Account Deletion Request. Include your registered account email in the message body. We do not delete accounts based on requests sent from a different email address.

3. Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Individual clues: delete items from the Capture screen in the app.
  • Local cache only: Settings → Clear local data (does not delete your cloud account or synced content).

4. What we delete

When your account deletion is completed, we delete or anonymize, as applicable:

  • Account identifiers (user ID, email linked to your account)
  • Work clues, notes, transcripts, and attachments you created
  • AI-generated reports and related metadata
  • Cloud sync snapshots and profile settings stored for your account
  • Voice recordings stored for your account

5. What we may retain (and why)

  • Legal, security, or fraud-prevention records — only where required by law or to protect the service; kept no longer than necessary.
  • Subscription/billing records — handled by Google Play or Apple; manage or cancel in store settings. We retain minimal entitlement status only as needed for support and compliance.
  • Aggregated or anonymized data — data that no longer identifies you may be retained for reliability and analytics.

6. Processing time

Account deletion initiated in the app is processed promptly. Email requests are typically completed within 30 days, often sooner, after we verify ownership. You will lose access once deletion is confirmed.
